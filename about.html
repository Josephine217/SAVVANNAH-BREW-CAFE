About Savanna Brew Café

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one espresso machine and a dream to celebrate the rich taste of Kenyan coffee.

Our founder, Mrs. Amina Otieno, grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri. Her love for coffee inspired her to create a welcoming café where people could enjoy quality coffee and fresh baked snacks.

Today, we proudly source our coffee beans from local farmers in Nyeri and Kiambu. We are committed to supporting local communities while providing our customers with a memorable café experience.

Our Values

Quality

We use freshly roasted, single-origin Kenyan coffee beans to provide excellent flavour in every cup.

Community

We support local farmers by offering fair prices and creating a welcoming space for everyone.

Sustainability

We care for the environment through compostable cups and zero-waste pastry practices.

"Brewed in Kenya. Served with love."

Every cup connects us to our farmers, our community and the beautiful Kenyan coffee heritage.
